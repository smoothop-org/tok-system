# tok-system

The public showcase of the **tôk system**, an alternative economic system
built on the principle of a universal basic income.

The tôk system's ABCDE:
- Accessible
- Beneficial
- Coherent
- Democratic
- Equitable

This repository, hosted under [smoothop-org](https://github.com/smoothop-org),
is the window onto the system: where its publications live and where the ideas are stated for anyone to read. It is a showcase, not a workbench. The experimental work happens elsewhere.

## Publications

The tôk system is developed through a series of documents. Planned:

1. **$tôkEx** — defensive publication (TDCommons), establishing dated prior art.
2. **Part 1 — time, umans, values, tôks and flows.**
3. **Part 2 — the democratic tax and the $tôkEx.**
4. **Part 3 — the citizen carbon exchange.**
5. **Part 4 — the socioecological transition: a model of what could happen.**

Each publication will live in its own subdirectory under `publications/`, named
by its subject, with its named authors.
